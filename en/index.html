Result

Enter values and select Calculate.

FORMULA

V_out = V_in R₂ / (R₁ + R₂)

Assumptions

Use R₁,R₂>0. Assume two series resistors, an ideal source and no output load. Significant ADC or other loading requires a different model.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Measurement uncertainty and design safety factors are not calculated.
